🌀 Cyclone Emergency Guide

Essential actions to stay safe during cyclones in Bangladesh

999 - Emergency
1090 - Cyclone Warning

⚡ Immediate Actions

🏠

Seek Shelter

Move to a designated cyclone shelter or strong building immediately

📻

Stay Informed

Listen to weather updates on battery-powered radio

⚠️

Evacuate if Ordered

Follow official evacuation orders without delay

🔍 Warning Signs & Preparation

1

Weather Warnings

Listen for official cyclone warnings (Signal 3-10) from Bangladesh Meteorological Department.

2

Prepare Emergency Kit

Pack dry food, water, medicines, flashlight, radio, batteries, important documents in waterproof bags.

3

Secure Your Home

Reinforce doors/windows, trim trees, secure loose items, and know your nearest cyclone shelter location.

4

Evacuation Plan

Plan evacuation route and transportation. Identify safe meeting points for family members.

🌀 During a Cyclone

1

Stay Indoors

Remain in shelter until official all-clear. Stay away from windows and in strongest part of building.

2

Power Safety

Turn off electricity at main switch if flooding occurs. Use flashlights instead of candles.

3

Flood Precautions

Move to higher ground if flooding begins. Never walk or drive through flood waters.

4

Monitor Updates

Keep listening to radio for cyclone position and emergency instructions.

🏠 After a Cyclone

1

Wait for All-Clear

Stay in shelter until authorities declare it safe. The eye of cyclone brings temporary calm.

2

Check for Injuries

Administer first aid if trained. Call 999 for serious injuries.

3

Safety Inspection

Check for structural damage, gas leaks, electrical hazards before re-entering buildings.

4

Boil Water

Assume water is contaminated. Boil or use purified water until authorities confirm safety.

5

Report Hazards

Report downed power lines, broken gas lines, and other dangers to authorities.

6

Help Neighbors

Check on neighbors, especially elderly or disabled. Work together for cleanup.
