🌪️ Tornado Emergency Guide

Quick actions to stay safe during tornadoes in Bangladesh

999 - Emergency
1070 - Disaster Control
1096 - Weather Updates

⚡ Immediate Actions

🏠

Seek Shelter Immediately

Go to the lowest level of a sturdy building, preferably a basement or storm cellar

🚪

Find Safe Spot

Stay in an interior room or hallway away from windows, doors and outside walls

🛏️

Protect Yourself

Cover yourself with blankets or get under sturdy furniture to protect from debris

🔍 Warning Signs & Preparation

1

Dark, Greenish Sky

The sky may appear dark and greenish, which is a sign of severe weather conditions that could produce tornadoes.

2

Large Hail

Large hail without rain can sometimes precede tornadoes, especially in the absence of thunderstorms.

3

Loud Roaring Sound

A loud, continuous roar or rumble that doesn't fade like thunder may indicate an approaching tornado.

4

Debris Cloud

A visible rotating cloud of debris can mark the location of a tornado even if a funnel is not visible.

5

Prepare Safe Room

Identify the safest room in your home (basement, interior room on lowest floor) and practice tornado drills.

🚨 During a Tornado

1

If in a House

Go to the basement or an interior room on the lowest floor. Stay away from corners, windows, doors and outside walls.

2

If in a Mobile Home

Get out immediately and go to the lowest floor of a sturdy nearby building or storm shelter.

3

If Outdoors

Seek shelter immediately. If no shelter is available, lie flat in a ditch or low-lying area and cover your head.

4

If in a Vehicle

Do not try to outrun a tornado. Either park the car and get into a sturdy building, or if no building is available, lie flat in a ditch away from the vehicle.

5

Protect Your Head

Use your arms to protect your head and neck, and cover yourself with blankets, coats or cushions if possible.

🏠 After a Tornado

1

Check for Injuries

Check yourself and others for injuries. Provide first aid if trained. Do not move seriously injured people unless they're in immediate danger.

2

Watch for Hazards

Be aware of broken glass, downed power lines, gas leaks, and structural damage. Wear sturdy shoes and protective clothing when walking through debris.

3

Turn Off Utilities

Turn off gas, electricity, and water if you suspect damage or if instructed to do so by authorities.

4

Listen to Authorities

Monitor battery-powered radio for emergency information. Follow instructions from local officials.

5

Help Neighbors

Check on neighbors, especially elderly or disabled people who may need assistance.

6

Document Damage

Take photos of damage for insurance claims before cleaning up. Contact your insurance company as soon as possible.
