🔥 Fire Emergency Guide

Quick actions to stay safe during fire emergencies in Bangladesh

999 - Emergency
16163 - Fire Service

⚡ Immediate Actions

🚪

Get Out Fast

Leave immediately if fire starts. Don't stop for belongings.

🧎

Stay Low

Crawl under smoke where air is cleaner. Cover mouth with cloth.

📞

Call for Help

Call 999 or 16163 (Fire Service) as soon as you're safe.

🔍 Fire Prevention Tips

1

Check Electrical Wiring

Regularly inspect for frayed wires, overloaded outlets, and faulty appliances. Replace damaged cords immediately.

2

Safe Cooking Practices

Never leave cooking unattended. Keep flammable items away from stoves. Turn pot handles inward.

3

Proper Storage

Store flammable liquids away from heat sources. Keep matches/lighters out of children's reach.

4

Smoke Alarms

Install smoke alarms on every floor and test monthly. Replace batteries yearly.

🚨 During a Fire

1

Evacuate Immediately

Leave the building as quickly as possible. Don't use elevators - take stairs instead.

2

Check Doors First

Feel closed doors with back of hand before opening. If hot, find another exit.

3

Stop, Drop, Roll

If clothes catch fire: STOP moving, DROP to ground, ROLL to smother flames.

4

Seal Yourself In

If trapped, close all doors between you and fire. Seal cracks with wet cloths.

🏠 After a Fire

1

Check for Injuries

Treat burns with cool water. Call 999 for serious injuries. Don't remove burned clothing stuck to skin.

2

Don't Re-enter

Wait for fire officials to declare building safe before entering.

3

Contact Utilities

Have gas, electricity, and water checked before turning back on.

4

Document Damage

Take photos for insurance claims. Keep receipts for temporary housing.

5

Seek Support

Contact local disaster relief organizations for assistance with housing, food, and clothing.

6

Emotional Care

Talk about the experience. Seek counseling if needed. Fires can be traumatic.
